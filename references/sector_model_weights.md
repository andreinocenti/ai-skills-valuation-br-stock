# Sector Model Weights

Use these method weights when consolidating evidence in the written analysis.

- DCF FCFE: 25%
- DCF FCFF: 25%
- Graham: 15%
- DDM/Bazin: 15%
- Multiples: 20%

Banks:

- Residual income: 35%
- P/VP and ROE: 30%
- DDM/Bazin: 20%
- P/L: 15%
- EV/EBITDA: 0%

Utilities:

- DDM/Bazin: 30%
- DCF FCFE: 25%
- DCF FCFF/EV: 25%
- Graham: 10%
- Multiples: 10%

Commodities:

- Normalized EV/EBITDA: 35%
- Normalized FCFF: 30%
- Multiples: 20%
- DDM/Bazin: 10%
- Graham: 5%

Retail:

- FCFF/FCFE: 35%
- Cash conversion and debt: 25%
- Multiples: 25%
- Graham/DDM: 15%

Holdings:

- SOTP/NAV: 60%
- Discount to NAV: 25%
- Dividends and cash flow: 15%
