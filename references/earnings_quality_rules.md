# Earnings Quality Rules

Mark quality warnings when:

- Net income is positive and free cash flow is negative.
- Operating cash flow is materially below net income.
- Payout exceeds 100% of adjusted income.
- A release mentions non-recurring items, asset sales, impairments, reversals, tax credits, provisions, FX effects or legal settlements.
- ROE is high because equity is unusually low.
- Commodity earnings are based on a peak-cycle year.

Quality levels:

- `high`: long history, cash supports earnings, no material one-off item.
- `medium`: sufficient data with some noise or sector cyclicality.
- `low`: short history, missing statements, negative equity, recurring losses or major one-off dependency.
